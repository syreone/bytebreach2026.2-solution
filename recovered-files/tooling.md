tooling
=======

notes on the kit, such as it is. mostly for whoever comes after me.

- the ledgers scan fine on the flatbed at 300. anything higher is vanity.
- the plate wants a cold light or the varnish flares.
- i didn't have an oscilloscope. You need to find the proper oscillosope.
- set the gain by hand. the ones that set it for you stretch tue ledgers out of shape,
  and then they read as something else entirely.
